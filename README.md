## Hi, I'm Nora

I'm interested in how AI thinks, talks, and gets misunderstood.

Not a developer. I explore from the language side — how things are named, described, explained, and how a product's words shape what users think it can do.

This is where I document what I'm learning and building.

---

### What's here

- **Prompt Lab** — prompts I've tested, with notes on what changed and why it worked. Less about hacks, more about understanding.

- **AI Product Notes** — observations on how AI products communicate. Trust, tone, capability signaling, failure language.

- **Building Log** — experiments using AI to make things. In-progress, sometimes broken.
